FAQ

Good questions.

Is it safe? Does it see my data?

It runs inside your browser tab using your own Swiggy Instamart session. There is no server and no analytics. Saved items and price history stay in your browser.

Why do I get no results at first?

On Instamart the tool collects what the page loads. Browse a category or press Auto-scroll first, then open Hunter again. Products appear as they are captured.

Is it official?

No. It is an independent tool, not affiliated with Swiggy Instamart. It only reads what your account can already see.

Will it keep working?

Swiggy Instamart can change its internal APIs at any time. The parser reads responses loosely to survive small changes, and the tool tells you when a scan returns nothing.

How does price-drop tracking work?

After each scan the tool remembers every price in your browser. Next time, new products are labelled New and cheaper ones Price drop.

Can I try it first?

Yes. Press Try the demo at the top to explore the full interface with sample data.
